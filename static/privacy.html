What we collect

  • Your email address, when you sign in with Google or with an emailed code. We use it only to sign you in.
  • When you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and whether Google has verified it. We don't receive your password, contacts or any other Google data, and we don't keep the token Google sends.
  • Your IP address is used briefly to limit how many sign-in emails can be requested, to prevent abuse. It is kept in memory only and not stored.

How it's stored

After you sign in, your browser keeps a signed cookie with your email address for up to 30 days. We don't keep a user database, don't use analytics or advertising trackers, and don't sell or share your data. Sign-in codes are sent through Amazon SES (Amazon Web Services), which processes your email address to deliver the message.

Your choices
